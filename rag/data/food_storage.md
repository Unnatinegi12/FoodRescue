# Food Storage

Proper storage helps donated food remain safe until it reaches an NGO or community kitchen.

## Refrigerated food

Perishable food should be kept refrigerated at or below 5 °C. Store cooked food in clean, covered containers and avoid leaving it at room temperature for long periods.

## Frozen food

Frozen food should remain frozen during storage. Do not redistribute food that has been thawed and left at unsafe temperatures.

## Separation

Keep raw food separate from cooked or ready-to-eat food. Raw meat, poultry and seafood should be stored separately to reduce the risk of cross-contamination.

## Containers and labelling

Use clean, food-safe containers. Donations should ideally be labelled with:

- Food name
- Preparation date and time
- Storage condition
- Donor information
- Any relevant allergen information

## Before redistribution

Check the food before handing it over. Do not redistribute food if the packaging is damaged, the food shows signs of spoilage, or its storage history is unknown.
