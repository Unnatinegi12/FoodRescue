# Food Handling

Good hygiene and careful handling help prevent contamination of donated food.

## Personal hygiene

People handling donated food should wash their hands before handling food and after activities that could cause contamination. Clean clothing and suitable protective equipment should be used when appropriate.

## Cross-contamination

Keep raw food, cooked food and ready-to-eat food separate. Use clean utensils and containers for different foods and avoid using equipment that has contacted raw food for ready-to-eat food without proper cleaning.

## Food preparation

Prepare food using clean surfaces and utensils. Avoid unnecessary contact with food using bare hands, particularly when handling ready-to-eat food.

## Checking food

Before donation, check the food for unusual smell, appearance or other signs of spoilage. Food with visible contamination or an uncertain history should not be redistributed.

## Allergens

When possible, record ingredients and common allergens in donated food. This information can help receiving organisations make safer decisions for people with food allergies.
