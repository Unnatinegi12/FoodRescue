# Food Transportation

Safe transportation is important because donated food can become unsafe if temperature and hygiene are not maintained during delivery.

## Temperature control

Keep hot food hot and cold food cold during transportation. Use insulated containers or suitable transport equipment to help maintain the required temperature.

## Transportation time

Keep transportation as short as reasonably possible. Food should reach the receiving NGO or community kitchen before its safe storage or consumption limits are exceeded.

## Packaging

Use clean, food-safe and leak-proof containers. Keep containers closed during transportation to protect food from contamination.

## Separation

Keep raw food separate from cooked and ready-to-eat food. Different types of food should be separated when necessary to reduce cross-contamination.

## Vehicle hygiene

The transportation area should be clean and protected from chemicals, waste, pests and other sources of contamination.

## Handover

At delivery, the receiving organisation should check the food condition, packaging and relevant preparation or storage information before accepting the donation.
